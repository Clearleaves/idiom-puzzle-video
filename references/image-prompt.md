# 原创谜面提示词

按当前成语填写下面模板。物体中心建议约在宽度的 15%、38%、62%、85%；宽大物品适当调整，保证完整和清楚。默认模板上方约 34%、下方约 32% 用于视频文字，不要添加多余装饰。

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create an original premium 9:16 vertical studio still life for a Chinese idiom puzzle video.
Exactly FOUR separate subjects arranged from left to right: [CLUE 1], [CLUE 2], [CLUE 3], [CLUE 4].
Warm ivory seamless backdrop, cream matte tabletop, soft warm sunlight from upper left, tactile materials, subtle realistic shadows, refined photorealistic 3D aesthetic. Any letter-like clue should be a freestanding golden Chinese character, spelled exactly as specified.
All four subjects completely visible, no overlap, no cropping, no duplicates beyond the explicitly requested ones, similar visual prominence. Keep subjects between 36% and 65% of image height, on the same baseline near 64%, with 6% side margins. The top 34% and bottom 32% must remain clean cream negative space for later video typography.
No title, no labels, no answers, no logos, no UI, no border, no watermark. No text except the explicitly requested sculptural character clues.
```

例如“十全十美”的主体描述：a freestanding golden Chinese 十, a clearly recognizable small Shiba Inu dog sitting upright with its full body visible, another identical golden 十, and a small dark ceramic vase holding sparse pink five-petaled plum blossoms.

不要仅用“一个动物”“一种花”等泛称。具体交代线索特征，并在成图后核查。生成模型可能偏离留白或字形要求，必须查看实际图片，必要时使用工具迭代。
